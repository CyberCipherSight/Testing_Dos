# Research backlog: SEO, AEO and GEO

**Status:** Future research — not a completed study.
**Goal:** Learn how search engines and AI assistants discover, retrieve, cite, and recommend open-source projects; develop a reusable, evidence-based publishing checklist for future projects.

## Questions to investigate
- How do classic indexing, ranking, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and AI answer citations differ?
- What affects visibility in Google AI Overviews/AI Mode, ChatGPT Search, Bing/Copilot, and Perplexity?
- What do crawler access, robots.txt, canonical URLs, structured data, GitHub README text, and independent references actually change?
- Which proposed GEO/AEO tactics have controlled evidence, and which are speculation or marketing claims?
- How can we measure indexing, referrals, citations, and recommendations without confusing correlation with causation?

## Research approach
Read official platform documentation first; then academic papers, independent experiments, technical articles, Medium posts, and developer community reports. Record publication dates, methodology, sample size, platform, limitations, and reproducibility. Separate verified platform requirements from hypotheses. Avoid keyword stuffing, fabricated testimonials, or unsupported compatibility claims.

## Future experiment
Choose several real, relevant queries (e.g., “online graphics.h compiler” and “Turbo C graphics programming in browser”). Record baseline search visibility and citations; improve documentation and indexing without changing the product claim; repeat measurements over time. Record whether the site was actually indexed and cited, and avoid interpreting a single AI response as a reliable ranking metric.

**Reminder:** Search inclusion and AI recommendations cannot be guaranteed. Test product functionality before promoting it.
